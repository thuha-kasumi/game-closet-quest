THE VERY SHORT VERSION

Good outfits.
Better company.

  1. Gather your people. Create a room and share its link or six-character code. Play with 2–6 people on separate devices.
  2. Dress for the occasion. You have two minutes. Pick clothes, add a caption, and lock your look. Everyone locks early? Reveal early!
  3. Meet the looks. Rate each other outfit from 1–5 for occasion fit and creativity. No self-votes. You have 90 seconds.
  4. Celebrate & repeat. A round score is the average of the ratings received, out of 5. Add scores across three rounds. Ties share the win.

Keep ratings kind and focused on styling. Missing votes are skipped; if a look gets no ratings, it earns 0 that round. The host moves everyone to the next round.

This first edition uses a shared illustrated wardrobe. Rooms are temporary and reset if the server restarts. No accounts, uploads, or AI services.
